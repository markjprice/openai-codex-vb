## Full description (both stores, 4,000 characters)

Puzzle Pocket puts three quick, classic puzzles on your phone. Play a round
while the kettle boils, beat your best time, and keep your winning streak
going.

THREE PUZZLES
• Noughts and crosses: play a friend on the same phone, or take on the
  computer. Undo a move whenever you change your mind.
• Sliding numbers: slide the tiles into order. Every board can be solved,
  and the timer only runs while you're playing.
• Word wheel: make as many words as you can from seven letters. Everyone gets
  the same new puzzle each day.

YOUR PROGRESS, ON YOUR PHONE
Puzzle Pocket remembers your games in progress, best times, scores, and win
streaks, and keeps them on your phone. It works without an internet
connection.

CALM BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, and nothing to buy
• No tracking or analytics
• Optional sounds and gentle vibration, which you can turn off
• An optional daily reminder, at a time you choose
• Works with VoiceOver, TalkBack, large text, and dark mode
