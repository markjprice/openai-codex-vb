# Puzzle Pocket beta: a guide for testers

Thank you for trying Puzzle Pocket before anyone else. It's a small
collection of classic puzzles: noughts and crosses, a sliding number puzzle,
and a daily word wheel. This is a test version, so you might find problems.
That's exactly what I'm hoping you'll do.

It should take about 15 minutes, spread over a day or two.

## Install the beta

**Android**

1. Open this link on your Android phone: [install link]
2. Tap Install. Your phone may warn you that the app isn't from Google Play.
   That's expected for a test version. Allow the installation for this app.
3. Open Puzzle Pocket from your home screen.

**iPhone** (only if I've sent you an invitation)

- If you received a TestFlight email, install TestFlight from the App Store,
  then open the invitation and tap Install.
- If I asked for your device details, open the link I sent you on your
  iPhone, and follow the steps it shows.

## What to try

1. Play each puzzle at least once:
   - Noughts and crosses, against a friend and against the computer.
     Try Undo.
   - Sliding numbers, until it's solved.
   - Today's word wheel. Try a few words that shouldn't count, too.
2. Turn on the daily reminder in Settings, and see whether it arrives the
   next day.
3. Partway through a puzzle, close the game completely (swipe it away in your
   phone's app switcher), then open it again. Does it remember where you were?
4. If you use large text, dark mode, or a screen reader, please try the game
   that way too.

## Three questions

1. What did you enjoy?
2. What confused or annoyed you?
3. Did anything go wrong? If so, what were you doing just before it happened?

## How to send feedback

Use the contact form on the Puzzle Pocket website:
[https://your site address/contact.html]

Choose **Problem** as the topic for anything that went wrong, and
**Suggestion** for ideas. Please include your phone's make and model, such as
"iPhone 15" or "Samsung Galaxy A15".

## What the game does with your information

Puzzle Pocket keeps your progress on your phone, and nowhere else. It doesn't
collect your name, email address, location, or contacts, and it has no
accounts, ads, or analytics. If you send feedback through the website, your
email address is used only to reply to you. The full privacy policy is at
[https://your site address/privacy.html].

## When the test ends

I'll let you know when there's a new version to update to, and when the test
is over. You can delete the beta at any time; deleting it deletes your
progress.
