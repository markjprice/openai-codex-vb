# Privacy policy outline

The headings a plain-language privacy policy needs, with notes on what to
write under each one. Write in short sentences, for players rather than
lawyers. Check every sentence against PRODUCT-BRIEF.md, and delete anything
the game or website doesn't do.

## Privacy

One or two sentences saying who the policy is from and what it covers: the
Puzzle Pocket game and this website.

## The short version

Three or four bullet points a player can read in ten seconds. For example:
the game keeps your progress on your phone, we don't collect your name or
location, and there are no accounts, ads, or analytics.

## The Puzzle Pocket game

- What the game stores, and where: progress, best times, win streaks, and
  settings, on the player's phone only.
- What the game doesn't collect: names, email addresses, locations, contacts.
- What the game doesn't have: accounts, advertising, analytics.
- Daily reminders: scheduled on the phone itself, and how to turn them off.

## This website

- No cookies, analytics, or advertising.
- Fonts and images are stored with the website, so visiting doesn't send
  information to other companies.
- The contact form: what it collects (a name, an email address, a topic, and
  a message) and that it's used only to reply.
- Where messages go: received through the hosting provider's form service
  (name it) and forwarded by email.
- What the hosting provider may record, such as internet addresses, to keep
  the site running securely.

## How long we keep messages

How long you keep contact messages, and when you delete them. Choose a period
you'll actually follow.

## Your choices

How people can ask to see or delete a message they've sent, and how quickly
you'll do it.

## Children

Whether the game is meant for children, and what that means for the
information you collect. Ask someone qualified for advice if you expect
players under 13.

## Changes to this policy

How you'll tell players about changes, such as updating this page and its
date.

## Contact

How to contact you about privacy: the Contact page, or a support email
address.

## Last updated

The date you last changed the policy, such as 3 November 2026.

---

**Watch out**: A privacy policy is a legal statement. Before you publish the
game, ask someone qualified to review it, especially if you expect players
under 13 or players in the European Union or the UK.
