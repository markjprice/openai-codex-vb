## How to Play

Page title: How to Play | Puzzle Pocket

Page description: Learn the rules of noughts and crosses, sliding numbers, and word wheel, and play a noughts and crosses demo in your browser.

### Headline

How to play

### Introduction

Each puzzle takes a minute to learn. Here are the rules, and a game of
noughts and crosses you can play right now.

### Noughts and crosses

1. Two players share one device. X always goes first.
2. Take turns to place your mark in an empty square.
3. The first player to get three marks in a row, a column, or a diagonal wins.
4. If all nine squares are full and nobody has three in a row, it's a draw.

### Try the demo

Section heading: Try noughts and crosses

Play with a friend on this device. Choose a square with a click, a tap, or
the Enter or Space key.

Restart button: Restart

Demo messages:

- Start of a game: X's turn
- After X moves: O's turn
- A player completes a line: X wins with the top row! (or the middle row,
  the bottom row, the left column, the middle column, the right column, the
  diagonal from the top left, or the diagonal from the top right)
- Board full, no line: It's a draw.

Square labels, for screen readers: Row 1, column 2, empty (or X, or O)

Move announcements, for screen readers: X in row 1, column 2.

No-JavaScript note: The playable demo needs JavaScript. You can still read
the rules above and play on paper.

### Sliding numbers

1. The grid holds numbered tiles and one empty space.
2. Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it into the space.
3. Keep sliding until the tiles are in order, from 1 in the top left.
4. Try to finish in as few moves, and as little time, as you can.

### Word wheel

1. The wheel has seven letters: six around the edge and one in the middle.
2. Make words of three letters or more. Every word must use the middle letter.
3. You can use each letter once in each word.
4. Find the word that uses all seven letters for a bonus.

## Support

Page title: Support | Puzzle Pocket

Page description: Answers to common questions about Puzzle Pocket, and how to get help.

### Headline

Support

### Introduction

Answers to common questions about Puzzle Pocket. If you can't find what you
need, send us a message.

### Questions and answers

#### Which phones does the game work on?

Puzzle Pocket is being made for iPhone and Android phones. We'll list the
exact versions it supports when the game is released.

#### Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Once it's installed, you can play every puzzle without an internet
connection, including on a plane.

#### How do I reset my progress or statistics?

1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
2. Choose Reset progress.
3. Confirm that you want to delete your progress.

This deletes your best times, win streaks, and scores for every puzzle. It
can't be undone.

#### How do I turn off the daily reminder?

1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
2. Turn off Daily reminder.

You can also turn off notifications for Puzzle Pocket in your phone's settings.

#### How do I turn off sounds and vibration?

1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
2. Turn off Sounds, Vibration, or both.

#### Is my progress saved if I change phones?

No. Your progress is stored only on your phone, so it doesn't move to a new
phone, and it's deleted if you delete the game. Puzzle Pocket has no accounts,
so there's nowhere else to keep it.

#### Is Puzzle Pocket free?

Yes. Puzzle Pocket will be free, with no advertising and nothing to buy in
the game.

#### How do I contact you?

Use the form on our Contact page. We usually reply within three days.

## Privacy

Page title: Privacy | Puzzle Pocket

Page description: How Puzzle Pocket and this website handle information, in plain language.

### Headline

Privacy

### Introduction

This page explains what the Puzzle Pocket game and this website do with
information. We've kept it short, and written it in plain language.

### The short version

- The game keeps your progress on your phone, and nowhere else.
- The game doesn't collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
- There are no accounts, ads, or analytics in the game or on this website.
- If you send us a message, we use your details only to reply.

### The Puzzle Pocket game

- Your progress, best times, win streaks, and settings are stored only on your phone. We can't see them.
- The game doesn't collect names, email addresses, locations, or contacts.
- The game has no accounts, advertising, or analytics.
- Daily reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

### This website

- This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or advertising.
- Fonts and images are stored with the website, so visiting it doesn't send information to other companies.
- The contact form collects your name (if you give it), your email address, a topic (if you choose one), and your message. We use them only to reply to you.
- Messages are received through Netlify Forms, the form service of our hosting provider, Netlify, and forwarded to us by email.
- Netlify may record technical information, such as visitors' internet addresses, to keep the site running securely.

### How long we keep messages

We delete contact messages, and the emails we receive about them, within
12 months of our last reply.

### Your choices

To see or delete a message you've sent us, use the Contact page or email
hello@puzzlepocket.example. We'll reply within 30 days.

### Children

Puzzle Pocket is suitable for all ages. The game doesn't collect personal
information from anyone. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian
before you send us a message.

### Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date below.

### Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our Contact page, or email
hello@puzzlepocket.example.
