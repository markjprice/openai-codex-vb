How to play

Each puzzle takes a minute to learn. Here are the rules, and a game of noughts and crosses you can play right now.

Noughts and crosses

  1. Two players share one device. X always goes first.
  2. Take turns to place your mark in an empty square.
  3. The first player to get three marks in a row, a column, or a diagonal wins.
  4. If all nine squares are full and nobody has three in a row, it's a draw.

Try noughts and crosses

Play with a friend on this device. Choose a square with a click, a tap, or the Enter or Space key.

The playable demo needs JavaScript. You can still read the rules above and play on paper.

Sliding numbers

  1. The grid holds numbered tiles and one empty space.
  2. Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it into the space.
  3. Keep sliding until the tiles are in order, from 1 in the top left.
  4. Try to finish in as few moves, and as little time, as you can.

Word wheel

  1. The wheel has seven letters: six around the edge and one in the middle.
  2. Make words of three letters or more. Every word must use the middle letter.
  3. You can use each letter once in each word.
  4. Find the word that uses all seven letters for a bonus.
