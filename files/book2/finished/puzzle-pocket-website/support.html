Support

Answers to common questions about Puzzle Pocket. If you can't find what you need, send us a message.

Which phones does the game work on?

Puzzle Pocket is being made for iPhone and Android phones. We'll list the exact versions it supports when the game is released.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Once it's installed, you can play every puzzle without an internet connection, including on a plane.

How do I reset my progress or statistics?

  1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Reset progress.
  3. Confirm that you want to delete your progress.

This deletes your best times, win streaks, and scores for every puzzle. It can't be undone.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

  1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
  2. Turn off Daily reminder.

You can also turn off notifications for Puzzle Pocket in your phone's settings.

How do I turn off sounds and vibration?

  1. Open Puzzle Pocket and go to Settings.
  2. Turn off Sounds, Vibration, or both.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

No. Your progress is stored only on your phone, so it doesn't move to a new phone, and it's deleted if you delete the game. Puzzle Pocket has no accounts, so there's nowhere else to keep it.

Is Puzzle Pocket free?

Yes. Puzzle Pocket will be free, with no advertising and nothing to buy in the game.

How do I contact you?

Use the form on our Contact page. We usually reply within three days.
