## Product type

Answer the questions in order, and stop at the first "yes":

1. Does anyone other than you need to use it? If not, a **script** may be enough.
2. Do people mainly read it? Choose a **website**.
3. Do people need it on their phone, without opening a browser, or with notifications? Choose a **mobile app**.
4. Otherwise, choose a **web app**.
